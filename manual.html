Overview

The cover. A schedule you can watch happen: milestones on one line, the turnaround below them, and — once you press Airborne — an aircraft that actually rides the curve.

On this screen

  • Schedule curve — STD/ETD/ETOT before departure, ETA/STA after. Once airborne, real times sit beside the planned ones with the delta between them.
  • Live local clock — under each field's name, ticking in real time off that airport's own UTC offset — the crew's own watch, not a plan figure.
  • Takeoff / Landing tiles — one tap each. Pressing Airborne rebases every time in the whole briefing off the real wheels-up.
  • Dispatch, Route, Documents — crew, aircraft, filed route and a link to the original OFP PDF.
  • Diversion — every filed alternate gets its own figures, live wind on its planned runway, and an “if you divert now” panel once you're flying (see below).
  • Watch items — every exception the app has flagged, worst first, moved to the bottom so it's the last thing you check before pushback.
ⓘThe countdown inside the curve counts past zero if you're running late — it never just freezes at 0:00.

Where the numbers come from

  • Uses your latest logged fuel reading when there is one, the plan's figure for your current position when there isn't — and always says which.
  • Over final reserve is what's left after landing at the alternate with reserve fuel still intact. It turns red the moment a diversion stops closing.
  • If more than one alternate is filed, each gets its own panel — the fuel math and wind are never only shown for the first.
  • Wind at the alternate is a live METAR resolved onto its planned runway. It's always marked approx. because it's read off the runway's number rather than SimBrief's own course figure — good enough to see a crosswind that's turned into a problem, not the number to fly an approach off.

Weather

METAR and TAF for departure, arrival and every alternate, plus SIGMETs and the charts SimBrief generated for the route. A live pull from VATSIM replaces the OFP's snapshot the moment it lands.

On this screen

  • WX / Charts tabs at the top — raw METAR/TAF on one, SimBrief's SigWx and winds-aloft images on the other.
  • Every airport gets three quick figures — temperature, wind, ceiling — before you read a single raw line.
  • A coloured dot shows flight category at a glance: green VFR through red LIFR.
  • The refresh icon beside the freshness note re-pulls VATSIM on demand; otherwise it refreshes itself every 30 minutes.
ⓘEvery bold token — wind groups, cloud, CAVOK, the lot — taps open. See Tap-to-decode.

On this screen

  • V1 / VR / V2 and the runway strip, exactly as SimBrief computed them for the planned runway.
  • The Now column only appears once a live METAR has landed — it resolves onto the same heading the plan used, so it's a true comparison, not just a fresher observation.
  • A note appears automatically when the wind has crossed a limit, or flipped a headwind into a tailwind, since the plan was built.
  • Every other runway is listed below, in case of a late change.

  • A lit green dot means someone is actually online there right now — not just that the frequency exists.

+

Tap-to-decode

Every bold token in a METAR, TAF, NOTAM or SIGMET — anywhere in the app — opens a plain-English definition right where you tapped it.

tap a tokendiagram
LEBL 151830Z 33008KT CAVOK 33008KT → From 330° at 8 kt

How it reads a code

  • Compound groups are parsed, not listed — 27015G28KT and 09007KT both decode correctly without either being memorised individually.
  • Covers wind, cloud, visibility, weather phenomena, altimeter, the TAF change indicators, and SIGMET's own vocabulary (SEV, TURB, EMBD…).
  • Close it by tapping it again, tapping anywhere else, or pressing Esc.
+

On a tablet, for the whole leg

Three things that only matter once the app is on a stand instead of a desk.

milestone alertdiagram
TOP OF DESCENTin0:05· 1634Z

Alerts

  • A banner and a chime for off-blocks, wheels-up and top of descent, timed off the real clock.
  • Turn on the bell in the rail to also mirror it to your system notification tray.
  • Dismissing one sticks — it stays dismissed even if the page reloads mid-flight — and a banner you never dismiss hides itself once its own window has passed.

Offline & the screen

  • Once loaded, the app keeps working with no signal — the shell is cached, and the last weather and traffic pull stays on screen with its age shown honestly.
  • The display is kept awake for as long as the briefing is open, so the fuel-check prompt is never missed behind a locked screen.
  • Live weather and VATSIM staffing refresh themselves every 30 minutes without asking.
  • Before wheels-up, the app quietly checks whether dispatch has regenerated the OFP since you loaded it. If so, a banner offers to reload — nothing changes on screen until you tap it.
